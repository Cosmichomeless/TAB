## Description
TAB splits shared expenses for trips, flats and nights out, privately.

There is no account to create and no server to trust. Your groups, expenses and balances live only on your iPhone, and the app contains no network code, no ads and no analytics.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Create a group in euros, dollars, pounds or yen and add everyone, even people who do not have the app.
• Record who paid and split equally, by shares, by percentage or by exact amounts. Everything adds up to the cent.
• See who owes whom and the fewest payments that settle the group.
• Settle up with shortcuts to PayPal, Revolut, Bizum or an IBAN. TAB never touches your money: it only records the payment.
• Share a group as a file, a QR code or a link, or paste a who-owes-whom summary into any chat.
• Back up every group to a file and restore it on a new iPhone.
• Erase everything from Settings whenever you want.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-up. No cloud. No tracking. TAB does not collect any data and asks for no permissions.

Available in English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and German.
